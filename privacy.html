Privacy

Last updated 23 November 2026

This notice explains what personal information Victorian Tiler collects through this website, why, and what your rights are. We keep it short and only collect what we need to answer your enquiry and do the job.

Who we are

Victorian Tiler is a floor and wall tiling business in London run by [Kieron's full name], a sole trader. Kieron is responsible for your information (the "controller"). You can contact him at Email@email.com or [phone number], or write to [business address].

What we collect

When you send the enquiry form we collect:

  • your name and email address, and your phone number if you give it
  • where the job is (for example a postcode or area) and what kind of job it is
  • when you'd like a visit, and what you tell us about the job
  • any photos you choose to add

If you email or message Kieron directly, we keep that conversation too. We don't collect anything else about you through the site, and we don't buy personal information from anyone.

Why we use it

Only to reply to you, arrange a visit, give you a quote and, if you go ahead, do the work. The legal basis is that you've asked us to take these steps before entering into a contract with you, and, once you're a customer, to carry out that contract. We also keep records because the law requires us to (for example for tax). We will never send you marketing or sell or share your details for anyone else's marketing.

Who else sees it

Enquiries are delivered to Kieron's Google account: the details arrive by email (Gmail), a visit request is added to his Google Calendar, photos are saved to Google Drive and a copy of the enquiry is kept in a Google Sheet. Google stores this for us as our service provider, under its own data protection terms, and may store it on servers outside the UK with legal safeguards in place.

The website itself is hosted by [hosting company, e.g. GitHub Pages], which, like any web host, may briefly log technical information such as your IP address to keep the site running and secure. The project photos are served from Google Drive. Nobody else receives your information unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

If an enquiry doesn't turn into a job, we delete it within [2 years]. If you become a customer, we keep your details and job records for as long as tax rules require (usually 6 years), then delete them.

Cookies

This site doesn't use cookies, analytics or tracking. It stores one small note in your browser for the current tab only (that you've already seen the opening animation), so it doesn't play on every page. It's deleted when you close the tab and isn't sent to anyone.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, ask us to stop using it or limit how we use it, or ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Just email Email@email.com. We'll reply within one month. It's free.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first so we can put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or 0303 123 1113.

Changes

If we change how we use your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
