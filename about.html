AboutKieron

Floor and wall tiling across London. Victorian path restorations, hallways, kitchens and bathrooms, laid by hand and finished properly.

How Kieron works

Kieron lays the patterned floors London's Victorian and Edwardian houses were built with: front paths, porches, hallways and balconies. Every floor is set out by hand, tile by tile, so the borders run true and the pattern meets the walls the way it should.

Most jobs start with a visit. Kieron looks at the space, checks what's underneath, talks through patterns and colours with you, and gives you a clear quote. If there's an old floor worth saving, he'll say so.

From preparing the base to cutting, laying, grouting and sealing, the whole floor is done properly, so it's ready for the next hundred years of muddy boots.

Service and Specialties

Pattern library

Choose from the classic Victorian geometric patterns: chequerboards, stars, diamonds and rosettes, framed with a border. Kieron brings samples on the visit and helps you pick colours that suit the house and the street.
